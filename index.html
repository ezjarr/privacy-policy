Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 30, 2026

ezjarrezjarr ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to protecting the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy outlines how our mobile applications handle user data in full compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and applicable global data privacy regulations.

1. Applicable Applications

This Privacy Policy applies to the following mobile applications developed and distributed by ezjarrezjarr:

  • Watermelon Party
  • FruityFive
  • FruityOthello
  • NeonPutt
  • MarbleRace
  • XMineSweeper

2. Collection and Use of Personal Information

Our applications do not collect, store, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII), including but not limited to real names, email addresses, phone numbers, physical addresses, biometric data, or payment information. Users can enjoy all gameplay features without any account creation or registration process.

3. Device Local Storage

Game progress, achievements, high scores, stage unlocks, and in-game preferences are stored strictly on your local device storage. This data is entirely controlled by you and will be permanently removed if you uninstall the application or clear app data via your device settings.

4. Third-Party Services & Network Communication

  • Leaderboard-Supported Games (Watermelon Party, NeonPutt, MarbleRace, XMineSweeper): To provide the optional global leaderboard ranking feature, these games communicate with Supabase database servers. The data transmitted and stored is strictly non-identifiable and limited to: a randomly generated anonymous user ID, a user-selected country code, and achieved game scores. No personal information or hardware identifiers are collected or linked.
  • Offline Standalone Games (FruityFive, FruityOthello): These games operate 100% locally on your device without any external network communication, backend servers, or third-party data transmission.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our applications are designed for general audiences and do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). If you believe that any information has been inadvertently collected, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt action to remove it.

6. Security

We prioritize the security of the minimal gameplay data handled. All network communications for leaderboard features utilize secure, encrypted HTTPS protocols.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our games, features, or relevant regulations. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
